\begin{spacing}{1.5}
{
指数增强是一类介于``完全跟踪指数''与``完全主动选股''之间的投资策略：它要求投资组合紧贴某一基准指数（本文以中国 A 股的沪深 300 为例），同时通过适度调整各只股票的持仓比例，系统性地获取略高于指数的稳定收益。实现这一目标的关键计算环节是``组合构建''——在已有个股涨跌预测的前提下，计算应给每只股票分配多少权重，并同时满足``不偏离指数太远、单只持仓不过高、行业配置均衡、交易不过于频繁''等一系列现实约束。该环节本质上是一个带约束的二次规划问题，它直接决定预测信号最终能有多少转化为真实可交易的收益，因而是整个策略能否落地的核心。

然而，要在以散户为主、噪声大、风格多变的 A 股市场上稳健地完成这一优化，存在两个层面的困难。其一在\textbf{求解层面}：优化需要估计股票之间收益的相关结构（协方差矩阵），但当股票数量与可用历史数据长度相当时，该矩阵难以估准、数值上高度病态，优化器会把估计误差误当作真实机会，给出极端而脆弱的持仓（即所谓``误差最大化''），且这种不稳定会在长达数千个交易日的连续回测中不断累积。其二在\textbf{输入层面}：传统优化只接收一个预测数值，默认所有个股的预测同等可靠，忽视了不同股票之间预测可信度的差异；在信噪比极低的 A 股，这会让组合把过多资金押在最不可靠的预测上。

针对上述两个困难，本文围绕指数增强的核心优化算法展开设计与实现，主要工作有两项。第一，面向 A 股设计约束型多因子组合优化算法：用 LightGBM 机器学习模型产生个股预测，用 Ledoit-Wolf 缩减估计与最小特征值修正抑制协方差病态，并以三级求解器回退机制保证长周期回测中数千次连续求解不退化、不中断。第二，针对``忽视可信度差异''的不足，提出不确定性感知的扩展：借助 Conformal Prediction（一种在很弱假设下即可为预测附带可信度、且具有覆盖率数学保证的方法）为每只股票的预测量化置信度，并设计两种将其纳入优化的稳健方案——把预测的不确定性作为额外风险计入优化目标，以及依据模型整体可信程度，在``信任模型预测''与``退守稳健分散''之间动态门控。

在严格的点位成分与去前瞻口径下，实证结果表明：长周期（2015--2024）上，所提算法将静态多因子基线的信息比率（衡量单位风险所获超额收益质量的指标）由 $-0.99$ 提升至 $+0.20$、最大回撤由 $-77.1\%$ 收窄至 $-39.4\%$；短样本（2024--2025）上，本文进一步发现 A 股环境下名义 $90\%$ 的 Conformal 预测区间实测覆盖仅约 $63\%$、且可信度与收益出现倒挂，因此简单地按置信度逐股加权并不可取，而所提的信度门控稳健混合方案能将不加权优化器的信息比率由 $-0.15$ 稳定提升至 $+0.50$（5 个随机种子全部由负转正）。本文代码与数据已开源：\url{https://github.com/aokimi0/IndexEnhancementStrategy}。
}

\end{spacing}

\begin{abstract}
\begin{spacing}{1.5}
Index enhancement is an investment strategy that sits between ``passively tracking an index'' and ``actively picking stocks'': the portfolio is required to stay close to a benchmark index (here, China's CSI~300), yet is allowed to tilt its per-stock holdings moderately so as to earn a steady return slightly above the index. The key computational step toward this goal is ``portfolio construction''---given predictions of how individual stocks will move, decide how much weight to assign to each, subject to real-world constraints such as not straying too far from the index, capping any single holding, balancing industry exposure, and limiting trading frequency. This step is essentially a constrained quadratic program; it directly governs how much of the predictive signal is ultimately turned into real, tradable return, and is therefore the crux of whether the strategy can be deployed.

Carrying out this optimization robustly on the retail-dominated, noisy, and style-shifting China A-share market poses two difficulties. The first lies at the \textbf{solver level}: the optimizer must estimate how stock returns co-move (the covariance matrix), but when the number of stocks is comparable to the length of available history, this matrix is hard to estimate and numerically ill-conditioned, leading the optimizer to mistake estimation error for genuine opportunity and produce extreme, fragile holdings (the so-called ``error maximization''), with such instability accumulating over thousands of consecutive rebalancing days. The second lies at the \textbf{input level}: a traditional optimizer ingests only a single predicted value and implicitly treats every stock's prediction as equally reliable, ignoring differences in trustworthiness across stocks---which, in the extremely low signal-to-noise A-share market, leads the portfolio to bet too much capital on the least reliable predictions.

Addressing these two difficulties, this thesis carries out the optimized design and implementation of the core optimization algorithm behind index enhancement, with two main contributions. First, a constrained multi-factor portfolio optimization algorithm for A-shares: a LightGBM machine-learning model produces per-stock predictions; Ledoit-Wolf shrinkage together with a minimum-eigenvalue correction suppresses ill-conditioned covariance; and a three-level solver fallback (OSQP$\to$CLARABEL$\to$SCS) keeps thousands of consecutive solves over long-horizon backtests robust and uninterrupted. Second, to remedy the neglect of per-stock confidence, an uncertainty-aware extension: Conformal Prediction---a method that attaches a confidence to each prediction under very weak assumptions and provides a mathematical coverage guarantee---quantifies the confidence of each stock's prediction, and two robust schemes incorporate it into the optimization: folding predictive uncertainty into the covariance as an extra risk term, and dynamically gating, according to the model's overall trustworthiness, between ``trusting the model'' and ``falling back to robust diversification''.

Under a strict point-in-time, look-ahead-free protocol, the empirical results are as follows. Over the long horizon (2015--2024), the proposed algorithm lifts the information ratio of the static multi-factor baseline (a measure of excess-return quality per unit of risk) from $-0.99$ to $+0.20$ and narrows the maximum drawdown from $-77.1\%$ to $-39.4\%$. On the 2024--2025 short sample, we further find that under A-share conditions the nominal $90\%$ Conformal interval attains only about $63\%$ empirical coverage, with confidence and return inverted, so naive per-stock confidence weighting is unwarranted; the proposed trust-gated robust blending instead lifts the no-weighting optimizer's information ratio from $-0.15$ to a stable $+0.50$ (turning positive across all 5 random seeds). The code and data of this thesis are open-sourced at \url{https://github.com/aokimi0/IndexEnhancementStrategy}.
\end{spacing}
\end{abstract}
